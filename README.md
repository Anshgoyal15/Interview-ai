# 🤖 InterviewAI

### AI-Powered Interview Preparation Platform

InterviewAI is an AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps candidates practice realistic interviews, receive personalized questions, and improve their answers with AI-generated feedback.
## 🎥 Demo Video

[▶️ Watch InterviewAI Demo on YouTube](https://youtu.be/P5NjkLpllA4)

## ✨ Features

- 📄 **Resume Analysis**
  - Upload your PDF resume
  - AI analyzes your skills, projects, experience, and education
  - Resume information can be used to personalize interviews

- 🎯 **Personalized Interview Questions**
  - Enter the company, role, and job description
  - Generate technical and behavioral interview questions
  - Questions can be personalized using your resume

- ⏱️ **Timed Interviews**
  - Practice with a 20-minute countdown
  - Answer questions one at a time
  - Automatic submission when the timer ends

- 🧠 **AI Answer Evaluation**
  - AI evaluates your answers
  - Receive a score and individual feedback
  - Blank answers receive zero points

- ✨ **Interactive Tutorial**
  - Built-in "How It Works" guide
  - Quickly understand the complete interview process
